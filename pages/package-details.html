Cruise palm-fringed backwaters on a private houseboat, wake up to misty tea gardens in Munnar and finish on a quiet beach. This trip balances sightseeing with time to slow down, and every transfer is private.

  • Private houseboat overnight
  • Tea plantation lunch
  • Kathakali show in Kochi
  • Beach resort stay at Marari

Day-by-day itinerary

Airport pickup, check-in and an evening walk through Fort Kochi's spice markets and Chinese fishing nets.

Scenic 4-hour drive past waterfalls to Munnar. Evening at leisure among the tea gardens.

Eravikulam National Park, the Tea Museum and Mattupetty Dam, with a plantation lunch.

Board a private houseboat with lunch, dinner and an overnight cruise on the backwaters.

Disembark after breakfast, relax at a beach resort and enjoy an Ayurvedic massage (optional).

Breakfast and transfer to Kochi airport. Your trip summary stays in your dashboard.
