Kerala in the Monsoon: A First-Timer's Guide

Why the rainy season is Kerala's best-kept secret, from quieter houseboats to lush tea estates and Ayurveda deals.

Sara Thomas, Honeymoon Specialist
28 Oct 2026 · 8 min read

Most travelers skip Kerala between June and September. That's exactly why the monsoon can be the best time to go: greener hills, emptier houseboats and some of the year's lowest prices.

Why visit Kerala in the monsoon?

The southwest monsoon arrives in early June. Rain usually falls in heavy bursts followed by clear spells, so most days still leave plenty of time for sightseeing. Hotels drop rates by 30–40%, and Ayurveda centres consider this the ideal season for treatments.

Where to go

Munnar

Tea estates turn an intense green and waterfalls appear along every hairpin bend. Carry a light rain jacket and leech socks for plantation walks.

Alleppey backwaters

Houseboats are quieter and cheaper. Rain on the canals, with a cup of chai on deck, is a memory most travelers rate as the highlight of their trip.

"The monsoon isn't a reason to stay away. It's Kerala at its most alive."

What to pack

  • Quick-dry clothes and a compact umbrella
  • Waterproof sandals with good grip
  • A dry bag for phones and documents
  • Mosquito repellent
