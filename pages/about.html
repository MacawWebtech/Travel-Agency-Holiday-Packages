A travel agency built by travelers. We plan handpicked holidays and stay with you from booking to the flight home.

Our story

From one backpack to 12,000 happy travelers

Wanderly started in 2012 when our founder Ananya planned a Kerala trip for friends and realised how hard it was to find honest, all-in prices. Today we're a team of 48 travel experts across Kochi, Delhi and Dubai.
